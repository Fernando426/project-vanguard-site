Privacy Policy for Project Vanguard

Last updated: September 12, 2026

Project Vanguard is an independent multiplayer video game currently in development. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be processed when players use Project Vanguard and its online features.

1. Epic Online Services

Project Vanguard uses Epic Online Services (EOS) to provide online features such as player authentication, online sessions, multiplayer connectivity, and related game services.

When you sign in using an Epic Games account, Epic Games may process information according to its own privacy policies and terms.

2. Information Used by Project Vanguard

Project Vanguard may receive or use identifiers provided by Epic Online Services, including an Epic Account identifier and/or an EOS Product User ID, as necessary to provide online multiplayer functionality.

During the current development and testing phase, Project Vanguard does not operate its own user-account database and does not intentionally collect passwords, payment information, or other Epic Games account credentials.

3. Multiplayer Information

Online multiplayer functionality may process technical and gameplay-related information necessary to create, discover, join, and participate in multiplayer sessions.

This may include session identifiers, player identifiers, connection information, game state, and other data required for multiplayer gameplay.

4. Data Storage

Project Vanguard currently does not operate a separate production backend for storing player profiles or personal information.

Information processed through Epic Online Services may be handled by Epic Games according to Epic Games' own policies and service terms.

5. Data Sharing

Project Vanguard does not sell personal information.

Information may be processed by service providers used to operate the game's online functionality, including Epic Games through Epic Online Services.

6. Children's Privacy

Project Vanguard is currently in development and is not intentionally designed to collect personal information directly from children.

Any future public release will be reviewed for appropriate age, consent, and privacy requirements before wider distribution.

7. Your Privacy Requests

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or wish to make a privacy-related request regarding Project Vanguard, contact:

Email: projectvanguard.privacy@gmail.com

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as Project Vanguard develops and new online features or services are added. The date at the top of this page will be updated when material changes are made.
